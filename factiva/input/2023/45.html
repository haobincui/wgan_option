1048 GMT - The Fed is broadly expected to take its last rate hike next month and then leave interest rates steady for some time. That could be a good time to buy oil, says JPMorgan. In all previous rate increase cycles since 1988, Brent crude oil has risen in the three months following the last hike, returning on average 9%, says Natasha Kaneva, the bank's head of global commodity strategy, in a podcast. "Buying the last hike has proven to be the correct strategy historically," she says. What happens next depends on the economy. Historically speaking, oil prices have turned negative as the U.S. economy entered a recession following rate hikes. But in the case of a mild recession, a new bull market in oil typically forms quickly, she adds. (william.horner@wsj.com)
