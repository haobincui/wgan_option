0832 GMT - Oil prices move higher after the Federal Reserve signaled that it would cut interest rates in 2024. Brent crude is up 0.9% at $74.91 a barrel while WTI is 0.8% higher at $70.02 a barrel. "Yesterday's FOMC meeting did its best to give investors an early Christmas present, all packaged with a bow and extra special gift wrapping. In turn this added more fuel to the soft landing narrative," Deutsche Bank's Jim Reid says in a note. Markets reacted positively to the news, and it now seems unlikely that the Fed will extensively keep rates high, he says. Deutsche Bank expects rate cuts to start in June, but says there is a risk cuts could come as early as March. (yusuf.khan@wsj.com)
