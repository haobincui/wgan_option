0921 ET - The stubborn overshoot in U.S. August inflation means that the Federal Reserve has still a lot of work to do, says Paolo Zanghieri, senior economist at Generali Investments , forecasting a 75 basis point interest rate increase by the Fed this week. That said, Generali Investments sees a significant risk that the Fed might go bolder, raising interest rates by a full percentage point, Zanghieri says. "After the 75bp rate [rise] in September we expect a somewhat slower pace of tightening, with a 50bps hike in November and a final 25bps one in December, with risks tilted to the upside," he says, expecting the Fed to keep the policy rate at 4%--upper end--until the beginning of 2024.
