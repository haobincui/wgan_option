WC 1488 words
PD 15 December 2022
ET 08:08

SC DJDN
LA English
CY Copyright © 2022, Dow Jones & Company, Inc.

LP 

0808 GMT - The Federal Reserve's new projections imply that the path to a soft landing scenario for the U.S. economy--meaning bringing back down inflation without triggering a recession--has narrowed further since September, Jefferies economists Aneta Markowska and Thomas Simons say in a note. The new economic forecasts show the U.S. economy is likely to suffer a higher pain threshold, with 2023 economic growth lowered to 0.5% from 1.2% previously expected, they say. The unemployment rate forecast was also up to 4.6% from 4.4%, meaning around 1.6 million jobs could be lost from the current levels, the economists say. "Avoiding pain isn't an option in the Fed's eyes. The goal is to minimize pain, and the sooner, the better," they say. (xavier.fontdegloria@wsj.com)
