1152 ET - A global economic recession doesn't look imminent, but risks will rise into 2023 and beyond, Oxford Economics chief global economist Innes McFee says in a note. Of the three major factors that could lead to a recession--the U.S. Federal Reserve 's policy tightening, the lockdowns in China, and high commodity prices--, none are expected to lead to a major global economic contraction, he says.
